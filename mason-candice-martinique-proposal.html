How We Work

Our Design Philosophy

At 1584 Design, we believe that exceptional spaces come from a deep understanding of aesthetics, architectural context, and functionality. Our designs honor the unique character of your home while creating beautiful environments that serve your specific goals - whether that's creating a welcoming space for renters or maximizing your property's market appeal.

Over the course of more than 30 years designing primary homes, second homes, and rental properties, we've seen over and over again how thoughtfully designed spaces have the power to elevate and enhance the experience of those who use them.

For rental properties specifically, well-designed spaces create memorable experiences for guests, leading to better reviews, higher occupancy rates, and increased rental income. They help properties stand out in a crowded market and justify premium pricing.

We tackle each project with this in mind, and our designs stand the test of time because we prioritize quality and timeless design principles over fleeting trends.

Our Process

  1. Agreement & Initial Payment

    Once we're aligned on this proposal, we'll take care of the Design Agreement that defines the terms of our working relationship and your initial payment.

  2. Design Planning

    Next, we'll create a Design Plan for your project with our ideas for the property and a plan to implement them.

    This includes identifying functional requirements for rental property success, spatial planning to maximize both aesthetics and functionality, choosing visual themes and color palettes that appeal to a broad range of renters, and selecting major design items that the rest of the design will be built around.

  3. Procurement

    We'll source and order all furniture, accessories, and essential items within your established budget. We'll utilize our trade discounts and sourcing expertise to maximize value while ensuring quality and durability suitable for rental property use.

  4. Implementation

    This is where everything comes together and the design comes to life. We'll coordinate delivery and placement of items, oversee furniture assembly, TV mounting, and all installation work. Our full-service approach ensures that every detail is executed with precision and care.

    From start to finish, we'll handle everything required to create a cohesive, polished result that's ready for your guests to enjoy.

Project Vision

Our design approach for this property centers on creating a warm, layered home rooted in the earthy, timeless neutrals that already reflect your personal taste. We'll establish a grounding foundation of rich browns, warm tans, and soft creams — intentionally stepping away from the gray-washed palette that dominates the short-term rental market — and layer in natural textures and materials that give each space depth, comfort, and a welcoming transitional sensibility that reads clean without feeling overly modern or old-world.

To bring the home to life, we'll thoughtfully weave in the colors you love like deep maroon, rich blues and oranges, and olive green — as considered accents against the neutral foundation. Each room will take on its own character and feel like its own experience, while a shared material palette and consistent design language keep the home cohesive from entry to bunk room. The upstairs great room and kitchenette will become a distinct hosting zone with a comfortable seating arrangement that makes the second floor feel like a retreat in its own right.

In the competitive vacation rental market, properties with real warmth and character consistently outperform those that rely on a safe, neutral-only approach. By grounding the home in earthy tones and weaving in rich, considered pops of color, we'll create spaces that photograph beautifully, stop guests mid-scroll, and leave lasting impressions that turn into repeat bookings and strong reviews. The result will be a fully furnished, rental-ready property positioned for success from day one.
