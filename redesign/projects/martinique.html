Martinique is a six-bedroom vacation rental in The Isles at Coral Canyon, planned for full houses and the rhythms of group travel. The brief reached across the entire property: gathering spaces that could comfortably hold a crowd, bedrooms with their own identity, and an outdoor area that felt like a natural extension of the home.

1584 Design carried the project from design planning through procurement, installation, and final styling. Hundreds of individual selections were coordinated into a consistent palette of warm neutrals, rust, olive, black, and natural texture—distinctive enough to photograph well, but relaxed enough to live with.

The result is a furnished, stocked, guest-ready property rather than a collection of separately decorated rooms. Furniture, lighting, rugs, window treatments, art, accessories, bedding, bath linens, kitchen essentials, and final placement were all handled as one cohesive installation.
