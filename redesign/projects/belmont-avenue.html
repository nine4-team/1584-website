Belmont Avenue

Project details

Property type
Vacation home
Location
Southern Utah

Belmont Avenue gives family and friends several ways to spend time together without asking one room to do everything. A long dining table and island seating support shared meals, while a separate theater and game room create dedicated places for movies, table tennis, arcade games, and quieter conversation. Bedrooms sit apart from those active rooms and use simpler furniture plans for rest.

The home’s architecture established a strong material direction. Wood-plank ceilings, dark cabinetry, stacked stone, blackened metal, and wide expanses of glass are balanced by leather seating, woven wall pieces, textured rugs, and substantial upholstered furniture. Sculptural pendants and large-scale artwork hold their own against the height and visual weight of the rooms.

Rust, olive, tobacco, cream, and charcoal recur across the shared spaces and bedrooms, but the rooms are not treated as copies. Brick, slatted wood, oversized art, and graphic sports references give individual bedrooms and activity spaces their own identities within the broader palette.
