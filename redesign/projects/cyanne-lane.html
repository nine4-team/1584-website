Cyanne Lane

Project details

Property type
Vacation home
Location
St. George, Utah
Community
Desert Color

Cyanne Lane takes a clean, high-contrast approach and softens it with warm wood, cognac leather, woven texture, and relaxed neutral upholstery. Graphic black accents create continuity throughout the home, while blush textiles and quieter bedroom palettes keep the overall direction from feeling severe.

The game areas use that same palette in a more casual way. A large neutral rug grounds the table-tennis setup, arcade games add brighter points of color, and lounge seating gives the room a place to pause between activities. In the bunk room, blush and cream bedding, rounded pillows, and a pale patterned rug soften the graphic black bed frames.

In the living and dining spaces, a charcoal sectional, carved wood console, black lighting, mirrors, and tables develop the contrast established by the palette. The bedrooms use upholstered beds, layered neutral bedding, wood nightstands, and restrained artwork for quieter variations. Outside, woven lounge chairs, deep-cushioned seating, dining furniture, and patterned textiles carry the same balance of structure and warmth onto the covered patios.
