A wall-size canyon photograph runs behind the dining table in this Arcadia Resort vacation rental. Landscape imagery reappears at different scales throughout the house: canyon photographs line the entry and bedrooms, national park prints flank the television in the upstairs lounge, and an oversized Zion National Park poster marks the built-in bunk room. Pale wood, caramel and rust upholstery, olive and ochre textiles, woven wall pieces, and rounded furniture carry that connection to the surrounding landscape beyond the artwork.

In the main shared room, the long dining table sits directly in front of the mural, while a cluster of woven pendants hangs over the dark kitchen island. A cream sofa, caramel lounge chairs, and a low drum table form another composition around the fireplace. Upstairs, woven wall pieces, park prints, shelves of earthenware, and rust-and-sage textiles continue the same materials and imagery.

The bedrooms develop those elements through specific combinations: one sets a canyon-stream photograph against floor-length ochre drapery and a heavy knit coverlet; another combines green drapery, canyon artwork, and a wall of woven baskets. The bunk room uses dark-stained timber, log-form stools, park imagery, and rust curtains. Covered patios extend the rounded and woven forms with lounge seating, geometric rugs, poufs, and a linear fire feature.
