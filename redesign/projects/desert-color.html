This Desert Color vacation rental was built around a bright, relaxed guest experience with enough color and personality to feel memorable without losing the calm that renters want when they arrive.

The property pairs open living and kitchen spaces with playful rooms that support group travel: game areas, bunk spaces, layered bedrooms, and outdoor areas that give guests more ways to gather throughout the stay.

Instead of treating the home as a set of separate rooms, the design connects the visual story of the listing with the practical needs of shorter-term renters—clear gathering zones, kid-friendly moments, comfortable sleeping spaces, and places to enjoy the Desert Color setting.
