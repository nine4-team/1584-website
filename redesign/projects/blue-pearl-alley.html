Blue Pearl Alley

Project details

Property type
Vacation home
Location
St. George, Utah
Community
Desert Color

Blue Pearl Alley takes a warm, nature-driven approach, combining organic forms, landscape imagery, and an earthy palette with more tailored furniture and lighting. Olive, rust, brown, cream, and ochre create continuity throughout the home, while changes in scale and composition give the shared spaces and bedrooms their own character.

In the main gathering space, curved cream sofas, olive swivel chairs, rust drapery, and a dark wood coffee table form a layered seating group beside a landscape mural. Brass-toned lighting, substantial wood dining tables, tailored chairs, and patterned rugs add structure, while olive, brown, cream, and ochre recur through upholstery, textiles, and accessories.

Wall-size mountain landscapes establish focal points in the living and dining rooms, and framed desert scenes and botanical artwork carry that connection to the landscape into the bedrooms. There, upholstered beds, layered neutral bedding, and wood furniture create quieter expressions of the same direction. In the bunk room, black-and-white bedding, compact tables, floor cushions, and playful wall details give the children’s space a more graphic identity.
