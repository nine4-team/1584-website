Common questions

Before We Begin

When should I bring 1584 Design into the project?

As early as practical. Early involvement gives us more influence over space planning, budget allocation, lead times, and the details that shape how the finished property performs.

What does full service include?

Design planning, sourcing, ordering, vendor coordination, delivery management, installation, and final styling. The exact scope is confirmed during project assessment.

Do you work outside Southern Utah?

Yes. We are based in St. George and serve the Zion corridor and Las Vegas, with select projects accepted nationwide.
