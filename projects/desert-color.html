Sensory Escape was designed with children and teens of varied abilities in mind, translating that inclusive intent into several kinds of play. The largest game room places an enclosed mini trampoline and foosball table alongside two hanging pod swings, a broad sectional, swivel chairs, books, and board games. In the bunk rooms, activity moves to a smaller scale through tactile wall boards, low floor seating, reading racks, and a bright green building-block wall.

Light wood, black metal, woven fibers, and softly textured upholstery recur through the main living room, kitchen, game spaces, and upper loft. Color is concentrated around the play features—orange on the trampoline and pillows, yellow and blue across bunk textiles and floor cushions, and green on the gaming chairs and building-block wall—while the other bedrooms use simpler furniture groupings, low-contrast bedding, and restrained landscape artwork.

The upper loft combines a console gaming setup, kitchenette, dining table, sofa group, and suspended chair. Glass doors lead to an outdoor lounge with sectional seating, dining furniture, a fire table, and a private hot tub overlooking the surrounding neighborhood.
